🔢📖 One Skill From Each Week

Click "Walk it through" on each one — Mr. Jonathan will manage the pace together with you.

Week 1: In 6,748, what is the digit 7 worth?
Answer: 700
1Line the number up by place value: 6 = thousands, 7 = hundreds, 4 = tens, 8 = ones.
2The 7 sits in the hundreds place.
3So the 7 is worth 7 × 100 = 700.
Week 2: 58 ÷ 9 = ___ (write remainders like this example: 9 R2)
Answer: 6 R4
1Ask: how many times does 9 fit into 58? Try 6: 9 × 6 = 54.
254 is the closest we can get without going over 58.
358 − 54 = 4 left over. So the answer is 6 R4.
Week 3: 2/7 + 3/7 = ___
Answer: 5/7
1Same denominator (7), so it stays 7 — we only add the top numbers.
22 + 3 = 5.
3So 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7.
Week 4: Which is GREATER, 0.8 or 0.65?
Answer: 0.8
1Give both the same number of decimal places: 0.8 becomes 0.80.
2Compare 80 hundredths to 65 hundredths.
380 > 65, so 0.8 is greater than 0.65.
Week 1: The problem or struggle a character has to face is called the ___?
Answer: Conflict
1Every story needs something to push against — a struggle the character has to deal with.
2That struggle has a name: the conflict.
Week 2: The particular way ONE character experiences or feels about an event is called their ___?
Answer: Perspective
1Two people can watch the exact same thing happen.
2How each of them personally sees or feels about it is their perspective.
Week 3: The thing that MAKES something else happen is called the ___?
Answer: Cause
1Ask yourself "why did this happen?"
2Whatever answers "why" is the cause. (What happens because of it is the effect.)
Week 4: The one big point that all the small details in a story support is called the ___?
Answer: Main idea
1A story can have lots of details, but usually one big point they're all supporting.
2That big point is the main idea.

🌟 This Unit's Vocabulary

In 5 × 8 = 40, what do we call the 5 and the 8?
Answer: Factors
1In 5 × 8 = 40, the 5 and the 8 are the factors — the numbers doing the multiplying.
In 42 ÷ 6 = 7, what do we call the 7?
Answer: Quotient
1In 42 ÷ 6 = 7, the 7 is the quotient — the result of the division.
3/6 and 1/2 look different but cover the same amount. What word describes fractions like these?
Answer: Equivalent
13/6 and 1/2 look different but cover the exact same amount — that makes them equivalent.
In 7.35, what do we call the dot between the 7 and the 35?
Answer: Decimal point
1In 7.35, the dot between the 7 and the 35 is the decimal point.
Specific facts or details someone gives to support their argument are called ___?
Answer: Evidence
1Evidence showed up the most this whole unit — Weeks 1, 3, and 4 all used it.
When both sides give up a little to reach a fair agreement, that's called a ___?
Answer: Compromise
1Neither side gets everything they wanted, but both sides agree to it — that's a compromise.
Closer Look 3 of 8

🔢 Numbers, Operations, Fractions & Decimals

31 × 14 = ___
Answer: 434
1Break both numbers apart: 31 = 30 + 1, and 14 = 10 + 4.
2Multiply every pair: 30×10=300, 30×4=120, 1×10=10, 1×4=4.
3Add them all up: 300+120+10+4 = 434.
52 ÷ 7 = ___ (write remainders like this example: 9 R2)
Answer: 7 R3
17 × 7 = 49, which is the closest we can get to 52 without going over.
252 − 49 = 3 left over. So it's 7 R3.
6/9 − 2/9 = ___
Answer: 4/9
1Same denominator (9), so it stays put.
26 − 2 = 4, so the answer is 4/9.
$3.75 + $2.60 = ___
Answer: $6.35
1Line up the decimal points: 3.75 and 2.60.
2Add cents first: 75+60=135, which is $1.35 — carry the $1.
3Add the dollars: 3 + 2 + 1(carried) = 6. Total: $6.35.
Closer Look 4 of 8

📖 Character, Perspective, Cause & Effect

If a character's "hands were shaking," what can you INFER?
Answer: Nervous or scared
1The author never says "she was nervous" directly.
2We use the physical clue (shaking hands) to figure out the feeling ourselves — that's inferring.
Two characters see the exact same event and feel ___?
Answer: Completely different things, and both can be valid
1Perspective means how a person personally experiences something.
2Two people can watch the same event and walk away feeling totally different — and neither one is "wrong."
It rained all week, so the outdoor game got canceled. What is the EFFECT here?
Answer: The game got canceled
1Cause = why something happened (the rain).
2Effect = what happened because of it (the canceled game).
The smaller facts and examples an author uses to PROVE the main idea are called ___?
Answer: Supporting details
1The main idea is the big point.
2The smaller facts that prove it are the supporting details.

👑 The Big One — Boss Battle Review

This was the final round mixing every story and every skill together.

A vendor sold 24 items at $15 each. 24 × 15 = ___
Answer: 360
1Break apart: 24 = 20+4, and 15 = 10+5.
220×10=200, 20×5=100, 4×10=40, 4×5=20.
3Add: 200+100+40+20 = 360.
A coach splits 42 water bottles evenly between 6 players. How many each?
Answer: 7
142 shared evenly between 6 people: 42 ÷ 6 = 7 each.
A student finished 7 of 10 homework problems. Write as a fraction.
Answer: 7/10
1"Out of 10" total, "7" done — that's 7/10.
Someone saved $22.40 one week and $15.60 the next. Total?
Answer: $38.00
1Line up the decimals and add: 22.40 + 15.60 = 38.00.
A student loses her house key and can't get inside before her parents get home. What would we call this problem?
Answer: Her conflict
1That's the problem she has to deal with — her conflict.
Reading the same event twice, once per character, practices what skill?
Answer: Perspective-taking
1Seeing one moment through two different characters' eyes is perspective-taking.
Two friends argue over whose turn it is to pick the movie, then agree to alternate every week. What is this an example of?
Answer: A compromise / resolution
1Both sides gave a little instead of one side losing completely.
A story has lots of details about training, sacrifice, and teamwork, all building toward one point. What do we call that one big point?
Answer: The main idea
1That's the big takeaway all the story's details were building toward.
Which word applies to BOTH comparing fractions/decimals AND weighing reading details?
Answer: Compare
1Compare shows up in math (which decimal is bigger) and reading (which detail matters more).
Which word explains WHY a character made a decision?
Answer: Motive
1The reason behind a choice is the character's motive.
Which operation does the phrase "shared equally among" signal?
Answer: Division
1"Shared equally among" is a division clue phrase.
True or False: A good summary should include EVERY detail from the original story.
Answer: False
1The whole point of a summary is to be SHORTER, keeping only what matters most — not every detail.
