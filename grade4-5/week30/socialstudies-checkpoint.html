Social Studies Final Checkpoint

Unit 6: Voices of Change. The civil rights movement, more voices for equal rights, the Cold War and the space race, and a changing nation: who pushed for change, and how do we know? This is the last stop of your 30-week KCE Pathways journey!

📋 Your Progress on This Lesson

🗺️ Today's Quest

  • Explain how ordinary people, including children, used courts, boycotts, marches and votes to change unfair laws
  • Show how farmworkers, tribal nations, women and people with disabilities organized, and how they learned from one another
  • Explain how the Cold War shaped Berlin, Cuba and space, and how it ended without the superpowers fighting each other directly
  • Use census numbers and three laws to show how new neighbors, new machines and new movements changed the nation after 1965
  • Face the Final Boss Battle and finish KCE Pathways Social Studies!
