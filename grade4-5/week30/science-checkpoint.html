🌍 Week 26: Gravity, Part 1

Remember: gravity is a pull between all objects with mass, and Earth’s pull points toward its center. Mass is the amount of matter and stays the same everywhere; weight is the pull of gravity on mass. On Earth, gravity pulls each kilogram with about 9.8 newtons, and the Moon’s gravity is about one-sixth as strong.

🌡️ Week 28: Heat and Thermal Energy, Part 1

Remember: temperature measures how fast, on average, particles move; thermal energy is the total energy of all the moving particles; heat flows from warmer to cooler until thermal equilibrium. Water freezes at 32 degrees Fahrenheit (0 degrees Celsius) and boils at 212 degrees Fahrenheit (100 degrees Celsius), and each Celsius degree equals 1.8 Fahrenheit degrees. Heat moves by conduction, convection and radiation.
