📰 Behind the Scenes of a Food Truck

Nonfiction Connection
📖

Real Talk: The Food Truck Industry

Food trucks are small businesses on wheels. Owners plan every ingredient ahead of time, because unlike a restaurant, they cannot easily "run to the store" mid-shift — once the line starts, there is no going back for more supplies. A single big event, like a festival, can bring more customers in one day than an entire normal week.

📖 The Line Around the Block — Part 1

Mia had never seen a line like this. It curled past the fountain, past the bandstand, and out of sight around the corner. Her stomach tightened as she counted heads — more people than she and Uncle Theo had ever served in a single day. "Uncle Theo," she said quietly, "did you see how many people are out there?" He glanced up from the grill, and for just a second, his jaw went tight before he forced a smile. "We'll figure it out, Mia. One order at a time."

+10

The text says Mia's "stomach tightened." What can you INFER she is feeling, even though the word isn't used?

🔍 How Inference Really Works

👁

Think About This

Authors don't always say "Mia felt nervous." Instead, they show it: a tight stomach, a held breath, tapping feet. Readers INFER the feeling by combining what's written with what they already know about how people act.

🧩

Worked Example

Clue: "Uncle Theo's jaw went tight before he forced a smile." What we know: forcing a smile usually covers up a real feeling underneath. Inference: Uncle Theo is worried too, even though he's trying not to show it.

📖 The Line Around the Block — Part 2

🌮

The Decision

Mia wanted to panic. Instead, she grabbed the order pad and did what her uncle always taught her: break the big problem into small steps. She counted the prepped tacos, checked the napkin boxes, and started calling out orders in a clear, steady voice. Customers who'd been grumbling about the wait started to relax when they saw how organized the truck actually was.

What is Mia's ATTEMPT to deal with the huge line?

📖 The Line Around the Block — Part 3

🌮

The Resolution

By the time the sun dipped behind the bandstand, the line was gone. Every single customer had been served. Uncle Theo wiped his hands on his apron and looked at Mia with real pride. "You know," he said, "the biggest days aren't won by never getting worried. They're won by staying steady once you are." Mia smiled — she hadn't stopped being nervous all day. She'd just kept going anyway.
