🔢 How Place Value Really Works

👁

Think About This

Every digit in a number has a VALUE based on where it sits. In 1,209,647, the 2 isn't just "a two" — it's sitting in the hundred-thousands place, so it's worth 200,000.

🧩

Picture It: 1,209,647

1 = one million · 2 = two hundred thousand · 0 = zero ten-thousands · 9 = nine thousand · 6 = six hundred · 4 = four tens · 7 = seven ones. Reading each digit's PLACE tells you its real value.

🌟 Vocabulary Round-Up

Flip each card to review today's business-math words.

factor
One of the numbers being multiplied together.
product
The answer you get when you multiply.
estimate

revenue
Total money earned from sales, before any costs are subtracted.
expense
Money spent to run the business — ingredients, gas, supplies.
profit
What's left after subtracting expenses from revenue.

🧮 The Area Model

👁

Think About This

To multiply two two-digit numbers, break each one apart by place value, multiply every piece, then add it all together. For 12 × 13: break 12 = 10+2, and 13 = 10+3.

🧩

Picture It: 12 × 13

The rectangle for 12 × 13 splits into four smaller rectangles by place value — one for each side-length pairing. Add up all four areas: 100 + 30 + 20 + 6 = 156. Four smaller, easier multiplications instead of one hard one.
