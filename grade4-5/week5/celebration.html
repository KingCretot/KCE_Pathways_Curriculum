🔢📖 One Skill From Each Week

Click "Walk it through" on each one — Mr. Jonathan will manage the pace together with you.

Week 1: In 3,482, what is the digit 4 worth?
Answer: 400
1Line the number up by place value: 3 = thousands, 4 = hundreds, 8 = tens, 2 = ones.
2The 4 sits in the hundreds place.
3So the 4 is worth 4 × 100 = 400.
Week 2: 36 ÷ 5 = ___ (just showing the format — write remainders like this example: 9 R2)
Answer: 7 R1
1Ask: how many times does 5 fit into 36? Try 7: 5 × 7 = 35.
235 is the closest we can get without going over 36.
336 − 35 = 1 left over. So the answer is 7 R1.
Week 3: 3/8 + 2/8 = ___
Answer: 5/8
1Same denominator (8), so it stays 8 — we only add the top numbers.
23 + 2 = 5.
3So 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8.
Week 4: Which is GREATER, 0.6 or 0.45?
Answer: 0.6
1Give both the same number of decimal places: 0.6 becomes 0.60.
2Compare 60 hundredths to 45 hundredths.
360 > 45, so 0.6 is greater than 0.45.
Week 1: A problem a character faces is called the ___?
Answer: Conflict
1Every story needs something to push against — a struggle the character has to deal with.
2That struggle has a name: the conflict.
Week 2: How one character sees or feels about events is called their ___?
Answer: Perspective
1Two people can watch the exact same thing happen.
2How each of them personally sees or feels about it is their perspective.
Week 3: The REASON something happens is called the ___?
Answer: Cause
1Ask yourself "why did this happen?"
2Whatever answers "why" is the cause. (What happens because of it is the effect.)
Week 4: The single most important point of a story is called the ___?
Answer: Main idea
1A story can have lots of details, but usually one big point they're all supporting.
2That big point is the main idea.

🌟 This Unit's Vocabulary

The numbers being multiplied together are called ___?
Answer: Factors
1In 4 × 6 = 24, the 4 and the 6 are the factors — the numbers doing the multiplying.
The answer to a division problem is called the ___?
Answer: Quotient
1In 36 ÷ 5 = 7 R1, the 7 R1 is the quotient — the result of the division.
Fractions that represent the same amount are called ___?
Answer: Equivalent
11/2 and 2/4 look different but cover the exact same amount — that makes them equivalent.
The dot separating whole numbers from decimal parts is called the ___?
Answer: Decimal point
1In 4.25, the dot between the 4 and the 25 is the decimal point.
Specific facts that support an argument are called ___?
Answer: Evidence
1Evidence showed up the most this whole unit — Weeks 1, 3, and 4 all used it.
An agreement where both sides give a little is called a ___?
Answer: Compromise
1Neither side gets everything they wanted, but both sides agree to it — that's a compromise.
Closer Look 3 of 8

🔢 Numbers, Operations, Fractions & Decimals

24 × 13 = ___
Answer: 312
1Break both numbers apart: 24 = 20 + 4, and 13 = 10 + 3.
2Multiply every pair: 20×10=200, 20×3=60, 4×10=40, 4×3=12.
3Add them all up: 200+60+40+12 = 312.
45 ÷ 6 = ___ (just showing the format — write remainders like this example: 9 R2)
Answer: 7 R3
16 × 7 = 42, which is the closest we can get to 45 without going over.
245 − 42 = 3 left over. So it's 7 R3.
5/6 − 2/6 = ___
Answer: 3/6
1Same denominator (6), so it stays put.
25 − 2 = 3, so the answer is 3/6.
$2.50 + $1.75 = ___
Answer: $4.25
1Line up the decimal points: 2.50 and 1.75.
2Add cents first: 50+75=125, which is $1.25 — carry the $1.
3Add the dollars: 2 + 1 + 1(carried) = 4. Total: $4.25.
Closer Look 4 of 8

📖 Character, Perspective, Cause & Effect

If a character's "shoulders felt tight," what can you INFER?
Answer: Stressed or under pressure
1The author never says "she was stressed" directly.
2We use the physical clue (tight shoulders) to figure out the feeling ourselves — that's inferring.
Two characters see the exact same event and feel ___?
Answer: Completely different things, and both can be valid
1Perspective means how a person personally experiences something.
2Two people can watch the same event and walk away feeling totally different — and neither one is "wrong."
What happens as a RESULT of a cause is called the ___?
Answer: Effect
1Cause = why something happened.
2Effect = what happened because of it.
Facts and examples that back up the main idea are called ___?
Answer: Supporting details
1The main idea is the big point.
2The smaller facts that prove it are the supporting details.

Which word matches with FRACTIONS?
Answer: Equivalent

Which word matches with MULTIPLICATION?
Answer: Factor
1Factors are the numbers being multiplied together.
Which word matches with a PERSUASIVE argument?
Answer: Evidence
1A persuasive argument needs proof to back it up — that proof is evidence.
Which word matches with two characters seeing the same event differently?
Answer: Perspective
1Different perspectives is exactly what makes two characters experience one moment differently.
Which word matches with finding a fair middle ground?
Answer: Compromise
1Fair middle ground, both sides giving a little — that's the definition of compromise.

👑 The Big One — Boss Battle Review

This was the final round mixing every story and every skill together.

Mia sold 18 items at $13 each. 18 × 13 = ___
Answer: 234
1Break apart: 18 = 10+8, and 13 = 10+3.
210×10=100, 10×3=30, 8×10=80, 8×3=24.
3Add: 100+30+80+24 = 234.
Zoe splits 50 stickers evenly with Milo. How many each?
Answer: 25
150 shared evenly between 2 people: 50 ÷ 2 = 25 each.
Marcus finished 10 of 12 chores. Write as a fraction.
Answer: 10/12
1"Out of 12" total, "10" done — that's 10/12.
Priya & Jordan saved $18.50, then $9.25. Total?
Answer: $27.75
1Line up the decimals and add: 18.50 + 9.25 = 27.75.
In Mia's story, what was her CONFLICT?
Answer: A long line of frustrated customers formed at her food truck
1That's the problem she had to deal with — her conflict.
Reading the same event twice, once per character, practiced what skill?
Answer: Perspective-taking
1Seeing one moment through two different characters' eyes is perspective-taking.
What was the RESOLUTION of Marcus's conflict with his dad?
Answer: A compromise — half a day Saturday if he finished chores that night
1Both sides gave a little instead of one side losing completely.
What was the MAIN IDEA of "Splitting the Profits"?
Answer: Fair doesn't always mean an identical split
1That's the big takeaway all the story's details were building toward.
Which word applies to BOTH comparing fractions/decimals AND weighing reading details?
Answer: Compare
1Compare shows up in math (which decimal is bigger) and reading (which detail matters more).
Which word explains WHY a character made a decision?
Answer: Motive
1The reason behind a choice is the character's motive.
Which operation does "how many are left" signal?
Answer: Subtraction
1"Left over" is a subtraction clue word.
True or False: A good summary is usually LONGER than the original.
Answer: False
1The whole point of a summary is to be SHORTER, not longer, than the original.
