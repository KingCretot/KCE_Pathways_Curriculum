🎓 Math Coach: Long Division, Step by Step

Step 0: Make a Multiples List

Before dividing by a 2-digit number, list its multiples. It turns guessing into looking.

32: 32, 64, 96, 128, 160, 192, 224, 256, 288

Divide → Multiply → Subtract → Bring Down

Example: 745 ÷ 32

1. Divide: 74 ÷ 32 → 2
2. Multiply: 2 × 32 = 64
3. Subtract: 74 − 64 = 10
4. Bring down the 5 → 105
1. Divide: 105 ÷ 32 → 3
2. Multiply: 3 × 32 = 96
3. Subtract: 105 − 96 = 9
Answer: 23 R9

Check: 32 × 23 + 9 = 736 + 9 = 745 ✅. The remainder (9) must be SMALLER than the divisor (32).

What Does the Remainder Mean?

Round up when every item needs a place (students need a bus). Drop it when only full groups count (full bags to sell). It is the leftover when it is money or items left behind (change after buying books).

The Bake Sale Split

Ms. Okafor's class held a bake sale and earned $360. The class agreed to give the money away, but they could not agree on where. Two students each wrote a short piece for the class to read before the vote.

Piece 1 · Lila: “Give It to the Shelter”

Our class should donate all $360 to the Sunny Paws Animal Shelter. The shelter cares for 15 dogs right now, so our money would give each dog $24 worth of food and care. The shelter director told our class that donations are lowest in the fall. Helping animals teaches us to care for something besides ourselves. A gift like this would make a real difference right away.

Piece 2 · Marcus: “Books That Last”

I love animals, but I think our $360 should buy new books for our school library. Each new book costs about $18, so we could buy 20 books. Our library has not gotten new books in 2 years, and many shelves are half empty. A dog's food is gone in a week, but a book can be read by hundreds of students for years. That is a gift that keeps on giving.
