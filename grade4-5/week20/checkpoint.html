🌾 Theme and Summary, Part 1

💡 The topic is what a text is about, in a word or two. The theme is its message about life, written as a full sentence. A summary tells only the most important events, in order, in your own words.

“The Oak and the Reeds,” an Aesop fable, retold

A tall Oak grew beside a river, where thin Reeds grew in the mud. “Why do you bow to every little breeze?” the Oak asked. “I never bend.” That night a great storm came. The Reeds bent low and let the wind pass over them. The proud Oak stood stiff against the wind until it cracked and crashed into the river.

Moral: It is better to bend than to break.
