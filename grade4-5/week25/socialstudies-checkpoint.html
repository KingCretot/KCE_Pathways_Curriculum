Social Studies Checkpoint: Unit 5

World War I and the Great Migration, the Harlem Renaissance, the Great Depression and World War II: who served and spoke up, and who was still denied freedom at home?

🗺️ Today's Quest

  • Explain why the United States entered World War I, who served, and why families moved north in the Great Migration
  • Use poems, essays and reports to show who shared in the 1920s and who still faced barriers, including in Tulsa
  • Explain how the New Deal helped Americans in the Great Depression, and who it left out
  • Show how Americans of many backgrounds served in World War II, and who was denied freedom at home and abroad
