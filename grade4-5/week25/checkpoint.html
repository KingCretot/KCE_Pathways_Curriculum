🦬 The Coordinate Plane, Part 1

💡 Walk before you climb: the x-coordinate (first number) tells how far right, and the y-coordinate (second number) tells how far up. Moving left or down subtracts. A point with x = 0 is on the y-axis; a point with y = 0 is on the x-axis.

🌕 Firsthand and Secondhand Accounts, Part 1

💡 Firsthand: the teller was there (I, we, my; feelings and close-up details). Secondhand: the teller was not there and used other sources (dates, numbers, the big picture). Always ask: firsthand about WHAT?

2From the club newsletter, written the next week by a parent who missed the launch: On Saturday, the Eastside Rocket Club launched 12 model rockets. According to the club’s records, the highest flight reached about 300 feet. Members said the parachutes opened on 11 of the 12 rockets.
