Real Talk: PortMiami

PortMiami sits on Dodge Island in Biscayne Bay, right next to downtown Miami. It is famous as the “Cruise Capital of the World,” and it is also a busy cargo port. 🖼️ Picture It: Inches, Feet, and Yards

👁

Same Length, Different Units

A yardstick is 1 yard long. It is also 3 feet long, and 36 inches long. The length does not change; only the unit does. When you convert, you are renaming the same amount with a different unit.

1 foot (ft)= 12 inches (in)
1 yard (yd)= 3 feet = 36 inches
Big unit → small unit: multiply  •  Small unit → big unit: divide
Example 1 — Big to small: A 20-foot container is how many inches long? Each foot is 12 inches, so you get MORE of the smaller unit: 20 × 12 = 240 inches.
Example 2 — Small to big: A crane cable is 15 feet long. How many yards? Every 3 feet make 1 yard, so you get FEWER of the bigger unit: 15 ÷ 3 = 5 yards.
Check your answer: converting to a smaller unit should give a bigger number, and converting to a bigger unit should give a smaller number.

⚖️ Weight and Capacity

🍌

Mr. Alvarez’s Fruit Containers

Mr. Alvarez checks refrigerated containers full of fruit. Fruit boxes are weighed in pounds, small items in ounces, and whole containers in tons. He also fills water coolers for the crew, and liquids are measured by capacity in cups, pints, quarts, and gallons.

Weight

1 pound (lb)= 16 ounces (oz)
1 ton (T)= 2,000 pounds
5 lb = 80 oz  •  3 T = 6,000 lb
Big to small: A bag of potatoes in the ship’s kitchen weighs 5 pounds. 5 × 16 = 80 ounces. A small truck weighs 3 tons: 3 × 2,000 = 6,000 pounds.
Small to big: A package of bolts weighs 64 ounces. Every 16 ounces make 1 pound: 64 ÷ 16 = 4 pounds.

Capacity

1 pint (pt)= 2 cups (c)
1 quart (qt)= 2 pints
1 gallon (gal)= 4 quarts = 8 pints = 16 cups
🧩

Step by Step

How many cups are in 2 gallons? Go one step at a time: 2 gallons = 2 × 4 = 8 quarts. 8 quarts = 8 × 2 = 16 pints. 16 pints = 16 × 2 = 32 cups. (Or use the shortcut: 2 × 16 = 32 cups.)

🌍 Metric Units: The Powers of 10

🏷️

Ms. Raman’s Cargo Labels

Ships come to Miami from all over the world, and most countries use the metric system. Ms. Raman reads cargo labels in meters, kilograms, and liters. The good news: metric units always change by 10, 100, or 1,000, so converting is quick.

1 meter (m)= 100 centimeters (cm)
1 kilometer (km)= 1,000 meters
1 kilogram (kg)= 1,000 grams (g)
1 liter (L)= 1,000 milliliters (mL)
💡

The Prefix Is a Clue

Kilo- means 1,000: a kilometer is 1,000 meters and a kilogram is 1,000 grams. Centi- means one hundredth: it takes 100 centimeters to make a meter. Milli- means one thousandth: it takes 1,000 milliliters to make a liter.

3 m = 300 cm  •  5 km = 5,000 m
Big to small: A ship’s gangway is 3 meters wide: 3 × 100 = 300 centimeters. The ship sails 5 kilometers up the channel: 5 × 1,000 = 5,000 meters.
More examples: 2 kilograms = 2 × 1,000 = 2,000 grams. 2 liters = 2 × 1,000 = 2,000 milliliters.
Small to big: A box of tools has a mass of 6,000 grams: 6,000 ÷ 1,000 = 6 kilograms.

🌟 Vocabulary Round-Up

Flip each card to review today's words.

convert
To change a measurement to a different unit without changing the amount. 1 yard = 3 feet.
conversion table
A table that shows matching amounts in two units, like feet and inches.
customary units
Units used most in the U.S.: inches, feet, yards, ounces, pounds, tons, cups, pints, quarts, gallons.
metric system
Units based on powers of 10: centimeters, meters, kilometers, grams, kilograms, milliliters, liters.
weight
How heavy something is. Measured in ounces, pounds, and tons.
mass
The amount of matter in an object. Measured in grams and kilograms.
capacity
How much a container can hold. Measured in cups, quarts, gallons, milliliters, or liters.
elapsed time
The time that passes from start to finish. 6:40 to 9:15 is 2 hours 35 minutes.

⏰ Time, Mixed Units, and Multi-Step Problems

🏗️

Aunt Guerline’s Crane Shift

A dockworker’s shift is planned in hours and minutes. Time converts just like other units: 1 hour = 60 minutes and 1 minute = 60 seconds. Be careful: an hour has 60 minutes, not 100!

3 hours 20 minutes = 200 minutes
Hours to minutes: 3 hours = 3 × 60 = 180 minutes. Add the extra 20 minutes: 180 + 20 = 200 minutes.
Minutes to hours and minutes: 135 minutes = 120 minutes + 15 minutes = 2 hours 15 minutes, because 120 minutes is 2 hours.

Elapsed Time: Count Up on a Number Line

Aunt Guerline starts at 6:40 a.m. and takes her first break at 9:15 a.m. How long did she work? Jump to the next full hour, then by hours, then the extra minutes.

20 minutes + 2 hours + 15 minutes = 2 hours 35 minutes.

4 ft 7 in + 2 ft 8 in = 7 ft 3 in
Step 1 — Add like units: 4 ft + 2 ft = 6 ft, and 7 in + 8 in = 15 in. That gives 6 ft 15 in.
Step 2 — Regroup: 15 inches is more than a foot. 15 in = 1 ft 3 in, so 6 ft 15 in = 7 ft 3 in.
🧩

Multi-Step Plan

1. Read the whole problem and find the unit the answer needs. 2. Convert so every amount uses the same unit. 3. Do the operations one step at a time. 4. Convert back if the question asks for a different unit, and check that your answer makes sense.
