🍍 Fruit from the Islands

Nonfiction Connection

Real Talk: A Hawaiʻi Farmers Market

Hawaiʻi is the only U.S. state made up entirely of islands. Its capital, Honolulu, is on the island of Oʻahu. The weather is warm all year, so farmers can grow fruit in every season, and farmers markets stay open year-round. The Kakaʻako Farmers Market in Honolulu is open every Saturday morning. A pineapple plant grows just one pineapple at a time, and it takes about two years to grow. Around the middle of the 1900s, most of the canned pineapple sold around the world came from Hawaiʻi. At the market you can also find papayas, lilikoʻi (passion fruit), macadamia nuts, and poke (say POH-keh), cubes of raw fish with seasoning. In Hawaiian, poke means “to slice or cut into pieces.” Many people finish their market visit with shave ice: a cup of soft, fluffy ice topped with fruit syrup.

🖼️ Picture It: A Decimal Divided by a Whole Number

👁

Equal Shares, Same as Always

Mr. Haddad has 4.8 liters of fresh pineapple juice and 4 empty pitchers. He wants the same amount in each pitcher. Dividing a decimal means the same thing as dividing a whole number: split the amount into equal groups.

4.8 ÷ 4 = 1.2
Think place value: 4.8 is 4 ones and 8 tenths. 4 ones ÷ 4 = 1 one, and 8 tenths ÷ 4 = 2 tenths. That makes 1 and 2 tenths, or 1.2.
Long division: Divide as if there were no decimal point, then put the decimal point in the quotient straight above the decimal point in the dividend. 7.56 ÷ 3: 7 ÷ 3 = 2 R1, 15 ÷ 3 = 5, 6 ÷ 3 = 2, so 2.52.
Check by multiplying: 2.52 × 3 = 7.56. It matches, so the answer is right.
⚠️

Two Tricky Cases

Placeholder zero: 0.24 ÷ 6. There are 24 hundredths, and 24 hundredths ÷ 6 = 4 hundredths = 0.04. The 0 in the tenths place holds the spot so the 4 stays in the hundredths place.
Write a zero to keep going: 5.4 ÷ 4 gives 1.3 with 2 tenths left over. Write 5.4 as 5.40 (the same amount) and keep dividing: 20 hundredths ÷ 4 = 5 hundredths. So 5.4 ÷ 4 = 1.35.

📚

Words in the Story

“Mr. Haddad divided 4.8 liters of juice into 4 pitchers, so each pitcher got 1.2 liters.” The dividend is the amount being divided (4.8). The divisor is the number you divide by (4). The quotient is the answer (1.2).

🔟 Dividing by 10, 100, and 1,000

🧪

Caleb’s Tasting Cups

Caleb wants to split 456 milliliters of guava juice into 10 tasting cups, then try 100 cups, then 1,000 tiny drops. Each time, the amount in one share gets 10 times smaller. Place value shows exactly what happens.

456 ÷ 10 = 45.6  •  456 ÷ 100 = 4.56  •  456 ÷ 1,000 = 0.456
The rule: Dividing by 10 moves every digit one place to the right, to a place worth 10 times less. Dividing by 100 moves every digit two places, and dividing by 1,000 moves every digit three places. The number of zeros in 10, 100, or 1,000 tells you how many places.
It works for decimals too: 37.2 ÷ 10 = 3.72, and 37.2 ÷ 100 = 0.372.
Fill empty places with zeros: 4.5 ÷ 100 = 0.045. The 4 moves from the ones place to the hundredths place, so a 0 must hold the tenths place. Also, 7 ÷ 1,000 = 0.007.
💡

Find the Pattern

60 ÷ 10 = 6.   6 ÷ 10 = 0.6.   0.6 ÷ 10 = 0.06. Each answer is one tenth of the one before it. Math writers sometimes write 100 as 10² (“ten to the second power,” meaning 10 × 10) and 1,000 as 10³ (“ten to the third power,” meaning 10 × 10 × 10). The small raised number tells you how many places the digits move.

💵 Money and Measurement

🏷️

Tutu Mahina’s Fruit Stand

Every Saturday, Tutu Mahina sells pineapples, papayas, mangoes, and macadamia nuts under her tent. Leilani helps find the price of one item, and Caleb changes metric measurements so the labels are easy to read. Both jobs use decimal division.

Money: Find the Unit Price

$31.00 ÷ 4 = $7.75
Unit price means the cost of ONE item. A box of 4 pineapples costs $31.00, so one pineapple costs $31.00 ÷ 4.
Estimate first: $32 ÷ 4 = $8, so the answer should be a little less than $8.
Divide: 31 ÷ 4 = 7 with 3 left over. Write 31 as 31.00 and keep going: 30 tenths ÷ 4 = 7 tenths with 2 tenths left, and 20 hundredths ÷ 4 = 5 hundredths. One pineapple costs $7.75. Check: $7.75 × 4 = $31.00.

Measurement: Small Unit to Big Unit

Going from a small unit to a big unit means dividing. Metric units change by 10, 100, or 1,000, so you can use the place value rule.

centimeters (cm) → meters (m)÷ 100: 250 cm = 2.5 m
grams (g) → kilograms (kg)÷ 1,000: 750 g = 0.75 kg
milliliters (mL) → liters (L)÷ 1,000: 1,890 mL = 1.89 L
millimeters (mm) → centimeters (cm)÷ 10: 45 mm = 4.5 cm
🧩

Does It Make Sense?

A kilogram is much bigger than a gram, so the number of kilograms must be much smaller than the number of grams. 750 grams is less than 1 kilogram, so 0.75 kg makes sense, and 7.5 kg or 75 kg do not.

dividend
The amount being divided. “Mr. Haddad divided 4.8 liters of juice into 4 pitchers.” 4.8 is the dividend.
divisor
The number you divide by. In 4.8 ÷ 4, the divisor is 4, the number of pitchers.
quotient
The answer to a division problem. “Each pitcher got 1.2 liters.” 1.2 is the quotient.
decimal point
The dot that separates the ones from the tenths. In the quotient, it goes straight above the decimal point in the dividend.
placeholder zero
A zero that holds an empty place. In 0.24 ÷ 6 = 0.04, the 0 in the tenths place keeps the 4 in the hundredths place.
place value
What a digit is worth because of where it is. Dividing by 10 moves every digit to a place worth 10 times less.
unit price
The cost of ONE item. “A box of 4 pineapples costs $31.00,” so the unit price is $7.75.
estimate
A close guess that helps you check. $32 ÷ 4 = $8, so $31.00 ÷ 4 should be a little less than $8.

🧠 Multi-Step Problems and Checking Your Work

🍹

Busy at the Juice Tent

Mr. Haddad fills big pitchers first, then pours each pitcher into small cups. That is two divisions in a row, and the second one is often by 10, 100, or 1,000.

12.6 L ÷ 6 = 2.1 L  →  2.1 L ÷ 10 = 0.21 L
The problem: 12.6 liters of juice are poured equally into 6 pitchers. Each pitcher is then poured equally into 10 cups. How much juice is in each cup?
Step 1: 12.6 ÷ 6 = 2.1 liters in each pitcher.
Step 2: 2.1 ÷ 10 = 0.21 liter in each cup. (Every digit moves one place to the right.)
Step 3 — Check: 0.21 × 10 = 2.1, and 2.1 × 6 = 12.6. It works backward, so it is right.
🚩

Watch Out for These Mistakes

  • Decimal point in the wrong place: 7.56 ÷ 3 is NOT 25.2. Estimate: 7.56 is less than 9, and 9 ÷ 3 = 3, so the answer must be less than 3. It is 2.52.
  • Missing placeholder zero: 0.24 ÷ 6 is NOT 0.4. Check: 0.4 × 6 = 2.4, not 0.24. It is 0.04.
  • Stopping with a remainder: 5.4 ÷ 4 is NOT “1.3 remainder 2.” Write a zero and keep going: 1.35.
  • Wrong number of places: dividing by 100 moves the digits TWO places, not one.
🧩

Multi-Step Plan

1. Read the whole problem and find what the question asks for. 2. Estimate so you know about how big the answer should be. 3. Divide one step at a time, lining up the decimal point. 4. Check by multiplying backward, and make sure the unit (liters, meters, dollars) is the one the question asks for.
