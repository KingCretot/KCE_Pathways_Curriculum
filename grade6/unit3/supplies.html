Grade 6 · Unit 3 · Weeks 11 to 15

Unit 3 Supply List

Almost everything comes from a normal home kitchen, pantry or junk drawer. Gather each week's items before Monday. Items marked optional can be skipped. Safety for Unit 3: never taste craft or test materials (yeast mixture, clay, paint, test bread); keep small pieces, paint and rinse water away from pets and young children; the Week 14 bread bags stay sealed and are thrown away unopened, and anyone allergic to mold should skip that test; outdoors, look but don't touch unknown plants and animals, watch for poison ivy, fire ants and snakes, wear gloves for leaf collecting, and wash hands afterward; never collect in state parks; an adult does any cutting.

Every week

  • A computer or tablet with a camera, microphone and the video-call app used for live lessons
  • A notebook or binder for each subject, pencils, an eraser and colored pencils
  • Index cards, graph paper and a ruler with centimeters and millimeters
  • A printer (or a local print shop) for printable pages and project labels

Week 11Life ScienceIs Yeast Alive? The Balloon Test
  • 3 packets (or 6 3/4 teaspoons) of active dry yeast
  • Sugar
  • Warm tap water (about as warm as a comfortable bath, near 105°F or 40°C) and cold water
  • 3 empty plastic bottles of the same size (about 16 to 20 ounces)
  • 3 balloons, stretched out first
  • A funnel or a rolled piece of paper
  • A measuring cup and spoons

  • Paper and pencil
  • Optional: a phone to photograph your tablet
