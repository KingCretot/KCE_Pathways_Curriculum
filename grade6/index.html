KCE Pathways: Grade 6

A full school year in ELA, Math, Life Science, and World and American History. Thirty weeks, six units, built for homeschool families and global thinkers.

How it works: each week has an interactive lesson for every subject, a printable practice page, a detailed teacher plan, and a parent guide. Every unit ends with a checkpoint, and ELA, Science and Social Studies end each unit with a project scored by rubric. Mastery is 85.
