Grade 6 · Unit 1 · Free to read or borrow

Unit 1 Book List

Every book here can be read free. The first group is in the public domain and free to read online. The second group is free to borrow with a public library card: look for it in your library's catalog or its free reading apps (such as Libby or Hoopla), or borrow a digital copy through Open Library when one is available. Availability varies by library.

Free to read online (public domain)

BookSubjectWhy read it
Mountain Interval
Robert Frost (1916)		ELAThe collection that includes "The Road Not Taken" from the Week 5 checkpoint.
The Weary Blues
Langston Hughes (1926)		ELAHughes's first book, which collected "Mother to Son" from Week 3.
Lyrics of Lowly Life
Paul Laurence Dunbar (1896)		ELAPoems by one of the first nationally famous Black American poets.
Anne of Green Gables
L. M. Montgomery (1908)		ELAA novel full of strong voice, dialogue and a character who changes.
Black Beauty
Anna Sewell (1877)		ELAA whole novel told in first person, by a horse. Good for talking about point of view.

Free to borrow with a library card

BookSubjectWhy read it
Brown Girl Dreaming
Jacqueline Woodson		ELAA memoir in verse about growing up Black in South Carolina and Brooklyn. A model personal narrative.
Hidden Figures (Young Readers' Edition)
Margot Lee Shetterly		ScienceKatherine Johnson and the Black women mathematicians at NASA, from the Week 5 lesson.
The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind (Young Readers Edition)

Lucy Long Ago: Uncovering the Mystery of Where We Came From
Catherine Thimmesh		Social StudiesHow scientists found and studied Lucy (Dinkinesh), from Week 3.

Jill Rubalcaba and Peter Robertshaw		Social StudiesFour famous fossil finds and how scientists argued about what they mean.
Sundiata: Lion King of Mali
David Wisniewski		Social StudiesA picture-book retelling of the griot epic from Week 1, good for a family read-aloud.
