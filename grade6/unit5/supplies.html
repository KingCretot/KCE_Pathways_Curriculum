Grade 6 · Unit 5 · Weeks 21 to 25

Unit 5 Supply List

Almost everything comes from a normal home kitchen, pantry or junk drawer. Gather each week's items before Monday. Items marked optional can be skipped. Safety for Unit 5: never taste or drink test materials (cabbage-juice indicator, bromothymol blue, food coloring water); an adult does all knife cutting, makes the cabbage juice with hot water and pokes holes with a pushpin or awl, and students may use scissors on paper; blow out through straws only, never suck in; never release aquarium plants or water outdoors (throw elodea in the trash); keep pennies, beans, blocks, sugar cubes, paper squares, game pieces and other small items away from young children (choking hazard) and pets; clear floors and peel up tape so no one trips, and keep a lamp nearby during the flashlight activity; on field visits, stay with an adult, keep well back from water where alligators may live, never touch or feed wildlife, and follow park rules about collecting; wash hands after every activity.

Unit 5 projects

  • ELA: The Call of the Wild, paper and markers or a device for the creative response
  • Science: a notebook for field data, a thermometer, a ruler or measuring tape, string and 4 stakes or pencils for a 1-meter quadrat, a phone or tablet for photos (optional), and poster board or a device for slides
  • Social Studies: poster board or cardboard for a game board, markers, index cards, a die and small items for game pieces

Every week

  • A computer or tablet with a camera, microphone and the video-call app used for live lessons
  • A notebook or binder for each subject, pencils, an eraser and colored pencils
  • Index cards, graph paper and a ruler with centimeters and millimeters
  • A printer (or a local print shop) for printable pages and project labels
