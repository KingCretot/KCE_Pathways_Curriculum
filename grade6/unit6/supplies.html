Grade 6 · Unit 6 · Weeks 26 to 30

Unit 6 Supply List

Almost everything comes from a normal home kitchen, pantry or junk drawer. Gather each week's items before Monday. Items marked optional can be skipped. Safety for Unit 6: an adult helps with scissors and hole punches; keep beads, buttons, beans, marbles, coins, sugar cubes, paper dots and other small items away from young children (choking hazard) and pets; don't eat foods used in activities, check for food allergies before tasting anything, and keep salt dough away from dogs and cats; visit stores only with a parent; survey only people a parent approves and never collect personal information; use only websites your parent approves; never dig at real archaeological sites or collect artifacts from public land; peel up tape and sticky notes gently and keep string off walkways; wipe up spills; wash hands after handling soil, seeds or food.

Unit 5 projects

  • ELA: The Call of the Wild, paper and markers or a device for the creative response
  • Science: a notebook for field data, a thermometer, a ruler or measuring tape, string and 4 stakes or pencils for a 1-meter quadrat, a phone or tablet for photos (optional), and poster board or a device for slides
  • Social Studies: poster board or cardboard for a game board, markers, index cards, a die and small items for game pieces

Every week

  • A computer or tablet with a camera, microphone and the video-call app used for live lessons
  • A notebook or binder for each subject, pencils, an eraser and colored pencils
  • Index cards, graph paper and a ruler with centimeters and millimeters
  • A printer (or a local print shop) for printable pages and project labels
