Grade 6 · Unit 2 · Weeks 6 to 10

Unit 2 Supply List

Almost everything comes from a normal home kitchen, pantry or junk drawer. Gather each week's items before Monday. Items marked optional can be skipped.

Every week

  • A computer or tablet with a camera, microphone and the video-call app used for live lessons
  • A notebook or binder for each subject, pencils, an eraser and colored pencils
  • Index cards, graph paper and a ruler with centimeters and millimeters
  • A printer (or a local print shop) for printable pages and project labels
