Grade 6 · Unit 4 · Weeks 16 to 20

Unit 4 Supply List

Almost everything comes from a normal home kitchen, pantry or junk drawer. Gather each week's items before Monday. Items marked optional can be skipped. Safety for Unit 4: never taste or drink test materials (food coloring water, vinegar, the digestion model, crackers used in models, road-model materials); an adult does all knife cutting and all cutting of bottles and balloons, and students may use scissors on paper; check for latex allergies before using balloons and keep balloons, small parts, gravel and bones away from young children and pets (cooked chicken bones splinter and can hurt dogs); use gluten-free crackers if anyone has a wheat allergy; keep vinegar away from eyes; chalk only on your own driveway or sidewalk away from traffic; run and toss balls only in clear, safe spaces; stop exercise and tell an adult if you feel dizzy, short of breath or in pain, and skip it if a doctor has advised against hard exercise; wash hands after every model.

Unit 4 projects

  • ELA: index cards and a phone or tablet to record the speech
  • Science: butcher paper (about 5 feet), markers, construction paper, red and blue yarn
  • Social Studies: poster board or a folded display board (or a device for four slides), markers and printed or hand-drawn maps

Every week

  • A computer or tablet with a camera, microphone and the video-call app used for live lessons
  • A notebook or binder for each subject, pencils, an eraser and colored pencils
  • Index cards, graph paper and a ruler with centimeters and millimeters
  • A printer (or a local print shop) for printable pages and project labels
