🔍 Research Spark

With a parent, be a scientist for one day. Look up your own town's climate: is it tropical, dry, temperate, continental or polar? Make a family safety plan and practice Drop, Cover, and Hold On together. Watch a live animal camera from a zoo or aquarium and look for animals working together. Visit the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission's IveGot1 website to see which invasive species live near you. You can also ask your library for Honeybee: The Busy Life of Apis Mellifera by Candace Fleming.
