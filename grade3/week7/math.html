➤️ Skip-Counting by 5s and 10s

Mia had 8 crates to price, each holding 5 apples. Instead of counting every apple one at a time, she skip-counted: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40. Eight jumps of 5 got her straight to the answer!

👁 Watch Me Think

Every number I land on while skip-counting by 5 is a MULTIPLE of 5 — and it also matches a multiplication fact. My 6th jump when counting by 5s lands on 30, which means 6 × 5 = 30. Skip-counting and multiplication facts are really the same thing, just two ways to get there.

🧩 Picture It: Skip-Counting by 5s

5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30
Jump 1=5×1, Jump 2=5×2 ... Jump 6=5×6=30

🤝 Let's Try Together

Skip-counting by 10s: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. The 5th jump lands on 50, so 5 × 10 = 50.

💵 Pricing Word Problems

Theo priced maple candy at $5 a bag. A hiker wanted 4 bags. “How much will that cost?” the hiker asked. Theo knew exactly what to do: multiply the price by the quantity.

👁 Watch Me Think

In a pricing word problem, I look for the PRICE of one item and the QUANTITY being bought. Then I multiply: price × quantity = total cost. Clue words like “each,” “per,” and “every” usually mean I should multiply.

🧩 Picture It: Price × Quantity

$5 per bag × 4 bags
5 × 4 = $20 total

🤝 Let's Try Together

Trail mix costs $10 a bag. A group buys 3 bags. 10 × 3 = $30 total.

⚡ Fast Facts, Fast Answers

By the end of pricing day, Theo and Mia could skip-count and solve word problems almost instantly — because they knew skip-counting and multiplication facts were the SAME skill, just used two different ways.

👁 Watch Me Think

When I get fast at skip-counting by 5s and 10s, I get fast at ANY word problem that needs those facts — because I already know where each “jump” lands. That's why practicing skip-counting makes pricing word problems easier, not just multiplication facts by themselves.

🧩 Picture It: Same Skill, Two Uses

Skip-count by 5, 7 jumps → 35
7 items at $5 each → $35 total

🤝 Let's Try Together

Fish hooks cost $5 a pack. A hiker buys 8 packs. Skip-count by 5, eight jumps: 5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40. Total: $40.
