🌟 Word Wrangler

Flip each card to learn this week's words!

regroup
To trade 10 of one place value for 1 of the next (like 10 ones for 1 ten) when adding or subtracting.
sum
The answer you get when you add numbers together.
difference
The answer you get when you subtract one number from another.
borrow
To regroup 1 ten into 10 ones (or 1 hundred into 10 tens) so you can subtract.
carry
To move a regrouped ten (or hundred) over to the next column when adding.

🏗️ Regrouping the Ones

When you add two numbers and the ones column adds up to 10 or more, you regroup: trade 10 ones for 1 ten, and carry that ten over to the tens column.

👁 Watch Me Think

Let's add 247 + 168. Start with the ones: 7 + 8 = 15. That's more than 9, so I can't just write 15 in the ones column — I regroup: write the 5, carry the 1 ten over to the tens column. Now the tens: 4 + 6 = 10, PLUS the 1 I carried = 11. That's still 10 or more, so I regroup again: write the 1, carry 1 hundred over. Hundreds: 2 + 1 = 3, plus the 1 I carried = 4. Final answer: 415.

🧩 Picture It: Regrouping the Ones

247 + 168
Ones: 7 + 8 = 15 → write 5, carry 1 ten
Tens: 4 + 6 + 1(carried) = 11 → write 1, carry 1 hundred
Hundreds: 2 + 1 + 1(carried) = 4
Answer: 415

🤝 Let's Try Together

Try 156 + 137. Ones: 6 + 7 = 13 → write 3, carry 1. Tens: 5 + 3 + 1 = 9 → no more regrouping needed there. Hundreds: 1 + 1 = 2. Answer: 293.

🏖️ Regrouping the Tens

Sometimes you have to regroup MORE than once in the same problem — once in the ones, and again in the tens. Just take it one column at a time, left to right doesn't matter, always go right to left.

👁 Watch Me Think

Let's add 385 + 267. Ones: 5 + 7 = 12 → write 2, carry 1 ten. Tens: 8 + 6 = 14, PLUS the carried 1 = 15. That's 10 or more again! Write 5, carry 1 hundred. Hundreds: 3 + 2 = 5, plus carried 1 = 6. Final answer: 652. Two regroups in one problem — totally normal, just do each column its own turn.

🧩 Picture It: Double Regrouping

385 + 267
Ones: 5+7=12 → write 2, carry 1
Tens: 8+6+1=15 → write 5, carry 1
Hundreds: 3+2+1=6
Answer: 652

⛰️ Subtracting with Regrouping

Subtracting works the opposite way. If the TOP digit in a column is SMALLER than the bottom digit, you borrow 1 from the column to the left.

👁 Watch Me Think

Let's subtract 342 − 168. Ones: 2 − 8? I can't do that, 2 is smaller than 8! So I borrow 1 ten from the tens column. Now I have 12 ones (2+10) minus 8 = 4. The tens column lost 1 ten, so it's now 3 (was 4). Tens: 3 − 6? Still can't do that! Borrow 1 hundred. Now I have 13 tens (3+10) minus 6 = 7. Hundreds column lost 1, so it's now 2 (was 3). Hundreds: 2 − 1 = 1. Final answer: 174.

🧩 Picture It: Borrowing

342 − 168
Ones: 2 is smaller than 8 → borrow 1 ten → 12−8=4
Tens: 3(now 3 after lending) is smaller than 6 → borrow 1 hundred → 13−6=7
Hundreds: 2(after lending)−1=1
Answer: 174

🤝 Let's Try Together

Try 523 − 267. Ones: 3−7? Borrow → 13−7=6. Tens: 1(after lending)−6? Borrow again → 11−6=5. Hundreds: 4(after lending)−2=2. Answer: 256.
