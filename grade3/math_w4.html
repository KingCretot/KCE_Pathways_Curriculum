🏗️ Addition Word Problems

A word problem tells a math story. The hard part isn't the adding — it's figuring out WHICH numbers to add. Look for clue words like total, altogether, or in all.

👁 Watch Me Think

“A trail has 145 pine trees and 168 oak trees. How many trees in all?” The words “how many in all” tell me I need the TOTAL — that means ADD. 145 + 168. Ones: 5+8=13, write3 carry1. Tens: 4+6+1=11, write1 carry1. Hundreds: 1+1+1=3. Answer: 313 trees.

🧩 Picture It: Finding the Clue Word

“How many in all?” → ADD
“How many altogether?” → ADD
“What is the total?” → ADD

🤝 Let's Try Together

“A hiking group has 234 adults and 156 kids. How many people altogether?” “Altogether” means add: 234 + 156 = 390 people.

⛳️ Subtraction Word Problems

Subtraction word problems use different clue words: left, remaining, how many more, or difference. These signal you need to find what's LEFT or the GAP between two amounts.

👁 Watch Me Think

“A trail is 420 steps long. A hiker has climbed 185 steps. How many steps are LEFT?” The word “left” tells me to SUBTRACT: 420 − 185. Ones: 0-5? borrow, 10-5=5. Tens: 1(after lending)-8? borrow, 11-8=3. Hundreds: 3(after lending)-1=2. Answer: 235 steps left.

🧩 Picture It: Finding the Clue Word

“How many are LEFT?” → SUBTRACT
“How many MORE do you need?” → SUBTRACT
“What is the DIFFERENCE?” → SUBTRACT

🤝 Let's Try Together

“A campground has 300 spots. 165 are taken. How many spots are left?” “Left” means subtract: 300 − 165 = 135 spots.

🌟 Estimating Sums

Sometimes you don't need an EXACT answer — just a close, reasonable guess. To estimate, round each number first, then add the rounded numbers. It's much faster than adding the exact numbers!

👁 Watch Me Think

Let's estimate 287 + 412. First, round each number to the nearest hundred: 287 rounds to 300 (since 87 is closer to 100 than 0). 412 rounds to 400 (since 12 is closer to 0 than 100). Now add the ROUNDED numbers: 300 + 400 = 700. That's my estimate — much faster than adding 287 + 412 exactly, and still a reasonable answer.

🧩 Picture It: Estimating Sums

287 + 412
Round: 287→300, 412→400
Add rounded numbers: 300+400=700
Estimate: about 700

🤝 Let's Try Together

Estimate 156 + 338. Round: 156→200, 338→300. Add: 200+300=500. Estimate: about 500.
