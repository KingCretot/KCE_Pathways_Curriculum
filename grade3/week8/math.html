🧩 What Is Division?

Mia had 24 trail bars and 6 baskets to fill — and every basket had to have the SAME amount, or it wouldn't be fair. She split the bars into 6 equal groups: 4 bars in each basket.

👁 Watch Me Think

Division means splitting a total amount into equal groups. I start with the TOTAL (24 bars), decide how many groups I need (6 baskets), and find how many go in EACH group. 24 ÷ 6 = 4 means “24 split into 6 equal groups gives 4 in each group.”

🧩 Picture It: Splitting 24 Into 6 Groups

24 bars ÷ 6 baskets = 4 bars in each basket
Basket 1: 4, Basket 2: 4, Basket 3: 4, Basket 4: 4, Basket 5: 4, Basket 6: 4

🤝 Let's Try Together

20 canteens split evenly into 4 crates: 20 ÷ 4 = 5 canteens in each crate.

🔄 Division Undoes Multiplication

Theo noticed something: he already knew 4 × 6 = 24 from Week 6. Mia's division problem, 24 ÷ 6 = 4, used the EXACT same three numbers! Multiplication and division are inverse operations — they undo each other.

👁 Watch Me Think

Every multiplication fact has TWO matching division facts, using the same three numbers. If 4 × 6 = 24, then 24 ÷ 6 = 4 AND 24 ÷ 4 = 6. These three facts together are called a fact family. Knowing my multiplication facts means I already know my division facts too!

🧩 Picture It: A Fact Family

4 × 6 = 24
24 ÷ 6 = 4
24 ÷ 4 = 6

🤝 Let's Try Together

If 5 × 3 = 15, then 15 ÷ 3 = 5 and 15 ÷ 5 = 3. Same three numbers, three related facts.

🛒 Division Word Problems

A hiking troop of 5 kids wanted to split 35 trail mix packets evenly to take home. Mia read the problem carefully: a TOTAL amount (35) being split into a number of EQUAL groups (5 kids). That's a division problem.

👁 Watch Me Think

In a division word problem, I look for the TOTAL amount and the NUMBER OF GROUPS (or the size of each group). Clue words like “split evenly,” “shared equally,” and “each” often signal division. Then I divide: total ÷ number of groups = amount in each group.

🧩 Picture It: Total ÷ Groups

35 packets ÷ 5 kids = 7 packets each

🤝 Let's Try Together

24 apples shared equally among 4 baskets: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 apples in each basket.
