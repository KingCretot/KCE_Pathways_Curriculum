🌟 Word Wrangler: Math + Reading Words

Flip each card. Say the word, then say what it means in your own words.

skip-count
To count forward by the same number each time, like 5, 10, 15, 20.
price
How much ONE item costs.
total
The full amount after adding or multiplying everything together.
point of view
The angle, or perspective, a moment is seen or told from.
infer
To figure something out using clues, without being told directly.
clue
A small detail that helps you figure something out.

📖 Read the Story

ReadingMath Read both parts. Part 1 shows the moment through Theo’s eyes. Part 2 shows the SAME moment through Mia’s eyes. Watch for the numbers!

The Price Tag Mix-Up

Part 1 · Theo's Side

“Price tags done!” Theo shouted. He had written every tag in record time. Water bottles: $5 each. Trail maps: $10 each. Trail bars: $5 each. Theo hung the tags on the shelves while Mia stacked boxes in the back room.

Soon a dad and his three kids walked in, hot and dusty from the trail. The dad picked up a water bottle and looked at the tag. His eyebrows shot up. He put the bottle back on the shelf, shook his head, and walked toward the door.

Theo's face got hot. “He didn't even say hello,” Theo thought. “He must think our trading post is junk.”

Part 2 · Mia's Side

Mia looked up from her boxes just in time to see the same moment. She did not see a rude customer. She saw clues. The kids were holding empty water bottles. The dad was counting dollar bills in his hand. When he read the price tag, he looked surprised, not angry.

“Something on that tag surprised him,” Mia thought. She walked over and read it. The tag said $50 each!

“Theo,” she whispered, “you wrote an extra zero!”

Theo fixed the tag to $5, and Mia hurried to catch the family at the door. “We're sorry! Our water is $5 a bottle, not $50!”

The dad laughed. “Now THAT is a price I can pay!” He bought 4 water bottles, one for each person, and 2 trail maps.

Theo grinned at Mia. “I only saw a grumpy customer. You saw the whole story.”
