🦋 The Butterfly's Life Cycle

Every organism has a life cycle — the stages it goes through from the start of life to becoming an adult that can have young of its own. A butterfly's life cycle has FOUR stages, and each stage looks completely different from the last.

👁 Watch Me Think

Stage 1: EGG — tiny, laid on a leaf. Stage 2: LARVA — hatches out as a caterpillar, and all it does is eat and grow. Stage 3: PUPA — forms a hard case called a chrysalis, and inside, its whole body rebuilds. Stage 4: ADULT — breaks out as a butterfly, ready to fly and lay its own eggs. Four totally different-looking stages, one life cycle.

🧩 Picture It: The Four Stages

EGG (tiny, on a leaf) → LARVA (caterpillar, eats a lot) → PUPA (chrysalis, rebuilding) → ADULT (butterfly, can lay eggs)

Then the adult lays new eggs, and the whole cycle starts again — that's why it's called a CYCLE.

🤝 Let's Try Together

Which stage eats the most? The LARVA (caterpillar) stage — that's its whole job, growing bigger before it changes shape. Which stage looks the LEAST like a butterfly? Also the larva — a caterpillar doesn't look anything like the adult it will become. That huge change in body shape is called metamorphosis.

🐦 The Frog's Life Cycle

Butterflies aren't the only animals with a dramatic life cycle. A frog's life cycle also has big changes — but the stages look very different from a butterfly's.

👁 Watch Me Think

Stage 1: EGG — laid in water, in a clump. Stage 2: TADPOLE — hatches out swimming, with a long tail and NO legs, breathing through gills like a fish. Stage 3: FROGLET — grows legs, and its tail starts shrinking. Stage 4: ADULT FROG — no tail at all, breathes air, can hop on land or swim in water. Same big idea as the butterfly — huge body changes — totally different stages.

🧩 Picture It: Two Life Cycles, Side by Side

BUTTERFLY: egg → larva (caterpillar) → pupa (chrysalis) → adult
FROG: egg → tadpole → froglet → adult

Different animals, different stages — but both go through metamorphosis to become adults.

🤝 Let's Try Together

Which frog stage looks the LEAST like the adult? The TADPOLE — no legs, a long tail, living only underwater, breathing with gills instead of lungs. Just like the caterpillar was the butterfly's "least adult-looking" stage, the tadpole is the frog's.

🐾 Life Cycles on the Trail

Not every life cycle has metamorphosis! A hiker who spots a baby deer (a fawn) on the trail will notice something different from a caterpillar: the fawn already looks like a small deer — four legs, ears, a tail — just smaller. It doesn't change body SHAPE as it grows. It just gets bigger.

👁 Watch Me Think

I compare the fawn to the caterpillar. Caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly is a HUGE shape change. Fawn → bigger fawn → adult deer is basically the SAME shape, just larger, with some new features like antlers added later. Both are still life cycles — egg or birth, growth, adulthood — but only one of them includes metamorphosis.

🧩 Picture It: Two Kinds of Life Cycles

WITH METAMORPHOSIS: butterfly, frog — body shape changes dramatically
WITHOUT METAMORPHOSIS: deer, dogs, humans, most birds — born looking like a small version of the adult, just grows bigger

Both are still life cycles — they just look very different along the way.

🤝 Let's Try Together: A Baby Bird in a Nest

A hiker spots a baby bird in a nest. Does it go through metamorphosis like a caterpillar? No — it grows feathers and gets bigger over time, but it doesn't change its basic body shape the way an insect or a frog does. Same idea as the fawn: growth without a dramatic shape change.
