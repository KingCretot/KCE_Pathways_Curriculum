🏕️ Basecamp: Place Value

You just found a trail sign: 452 feet to the summit. Every digit in that number has a place (where it sits) and a value (what it's worth because of where it sits).

👁 Watch Me Think

I read 452 left to right. 4 is three spots in — the hundreds place — so it's worth 400. 5 is next — tens place — worth 50. 2 is last — ones place — worth just 2.

400 + 50 + 2 = 452. Same digits, different jobs, depending on where they sit.

🧩 Picture It: Trail Mix Bags

Your backpack has bags of trail mix: 4 BIG bags (100 pieces each), 5 MEDIUM bags (10 pieces each), 2 loose pieces.
[100][100][100][100] + [10][10][10][10][10] + [1][1]
= 400 + 50 + 2 = 452 pieces

🤝 Let's Try Together: 926 feet to the ridge

9 is in the hundreds place — worth 900. Your turn: 2 is in the tens place. What's it worth? (2 tens = 20.) 6 is in the ones place — worth 6. 900 + 20 + 6 = 926. You've got it!

💡 Same digit, different value: the 4 in 452 is worth 400. The 4 in 249 is worth only 40. It's all about WHERE it sits.

🗺️ Packing the Number

Standard form is how you normally write a number: 736. Expanded form unpacks it so you see what every piece is worth: 700 + 30 + 6.

👁 Watch Me Think

736: the 7 is worth 700, the 3 is worth 30, the 6 is worth 6. Write those with plus signs: 700 + 30 + 6. Same number, just spread out.

🧩 Picture It: Unpacking Your Backpack

PACKED (standard): 736
UNPACKED (expanded): 700 + 30 + 6

Standard form is your packed bag. Expanded form is everything laid out on the ground so you can see each piece.

🤝 Let's Try Together: 305 feet to the next stop

3 is in the hundreds place → worth 300. Your turn: what's in the tens place? A 0 — worth 0, but we still WRITE it. 5 is in the ones place — worth 5. So: 300 + 0 + 5.

💡 Even a 0 gets written. Leaving it out would make the number confusing.

📍 Rounding on the Trail

To round to the nearest ten: look at the ones digit. 5 or more rounds UP. 4 or less stays DOWN.

👁 Watch Me Think

Round 47. It's between 40 and 50. The ones digit is 7 — that's 5 or more, so it rounds UP to 50. Now round 42: also between 40 and 50, but its ones digit is 2 — less than 5, so it stays at 40.

🧩 Picture It: The Trail Between Camps

40 -----42----(45)----47----- 50
42 is closer to 40 → rounds DOWN
47 is closer to 50 → rounds UP

Whichever camp a number is CLOSER to is the one it rounds to.

🤝 Let's Try Together: Rounding to the Nearest HUNDRED

Same rule, but now look at the TENS digit. Round 350: it's between 300 and 400. Tens digit is 5 — rounds UP to 400. Your turn: 320 is also between 300 and 400. Tens digit is 2 — rounds DOWN, stays at 300.
