🌟 Word Wrangler — Round Two

Flip each card. Five are old friends from last time — one is brand new!

multiply
To combine equal groups quickly, instead of adding them one at a time.
array
Items arranged in equal rows and columns.
equal groups
Groups that all have the exact same number of items inside.
product
The answer you get when you multiply two numbers.
repeated addition
Adding the same number over and over, like 4+4+4.
skip-count 🆕
Counting forward by the SAME number each time — like 5, 10, 15, 20.
